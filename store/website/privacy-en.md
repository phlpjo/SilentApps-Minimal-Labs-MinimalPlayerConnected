# Privacy – Minimal Player Connected

**In short:** Minimal Player Connected collects no personal data, has no servers and shares nothing with third parties. No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics or crash SDKs.

## What is stored on your phone

- the folders you picked for music and audiobooks and an index of the audio files in them
- playlists, saved positions and bookmarks
- tracks from your PC that you made available offline
- the address of your PC on your home network and your settings

None of it leaves the device unless you share it yourself via the Android share menu. If Android device backup is turned on, Android backs up the app data like for any other app – you control that in the Android settings.

## Internet

The app connects **only to devices on your own Wi-Fi**: your PC running Minimal Library and speakers/DLNA devices you choose. There is no server, no cloud and no connection to the developer or third parties.

## Permissions

| Permission | What for | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| Folder access (folders you pick) | play your audio files | yes |
| `INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, `CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE` | find and talk to devices on your home network | yes |
| Foreground service (media playback) | playback with the screen off | yes |

## Deleting

Uninstalling the app or “Clear data” in the Android app settings removes everything completely.
